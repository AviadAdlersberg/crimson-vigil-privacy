Crimson Vigil

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

Overview

Crimson Vigil ("the game," "we," "us") is a gothic vampire idle role-playing game developed as an independent project. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the game does — and does not — collect.

Data We Do Not Collect

Crimson Vigil does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Account or login credentials
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Contacts or photos
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to any server
  • Payment or financial information

Local Save Data

Your game progress (blood collected, upgrades purchased, current era, and other in-game state) is saved locally on your device using standard browser local storage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server or third party. You can clear your save data at any time by clearing the app's local storage through your device settings.

No Ads or Third-Party SDKs

Crimson Vigil contains no advertisements and does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. No data is shared with any third party for any purpose.

Internet Permission

The Android version of the game may request internet permission as part of the WebView framework used to run the game. No network requests are made by the game itself. No data is sent or received over the internet during normal gameplay.

Children's Privacy

Crimson Vigil is rated Everyone 10+ and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any other users. Because no data is collected at all, there is no special handling required for users of any age.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes in the future, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised "last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please reach out through the Google Play Store listing for Crimson Vigil.
